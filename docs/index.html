The 5 Challenge Prompts

Each participant is randomly assigned one challenge at T-0. All 5 challenges are revealed simultaneously when the hackathon begins.

Schedule

Competition Timeline

T+0h → T+2h Research & Ideation
Challenges revealed. Define the problem, research existing solutions, sketch concepts. Write your Problem Statement.
T+2h → T+10h Engineering Design
Electrical schematic, component selection, power budget, CAD / enclosure concept. Produce deliverables 2, 3, and 4.
T+12h ⚡ THE TWIST Live Vote
Participants vote on one constraint to apply to ALL designs for the remaining 12 hours. Voting window: 30 minutes. React to the pinned GitHub issue to cast your vote.
T+10h → T+18h Prototype & Simulation
Wokwi / LTspice / Falstad simulation, validate your architecture, produce deliverable 6. Incorporate the winning twist constraint.
T+18h → T+24h Packaging & Pitch
Polish documentation, create pitch deck (deliverable 7), push all deliverables. Final commit must be before T+24h.
Hour 12

The Twist

⏳
Twist Vote Opens at T+12h

16 constraints are on the table. You'll vote by reacting to the pinned GitHub issue.

At Hour 12, participants vote on one constraint that applies to all designs for the final 12 hours. The winning twist is applied immediately and must be reflected in all deliverables.

Vote by reacting 👍 on the constraint comment in the pinned GitHub issue →

Requirements

7 Required Deliverables

01
Problem Statement
Problem definition, user stories, existing solutions analysis, and gap analysis.
01-problem-statement.md
02
System Architecture Diagram
Block diagram, signal flow, subsystem breakdown. Can be image or markdown.
02-system-architecture.md / .png
03
Electrical Design
Schematic, component selection with justification, and power budget.
03-electrical-design.md
04
Mechanical Design
CAD files or screenshots, exploded view, enclosure concept and assembly strategy.
04-mechanical-design.md
05
Firmware Logic
Pseudocode, real code, state machine, or flowchart. Wokwi simulation link welcome.
05-firmware-logic.md / .ino / .py
06
Simulation / Validation
Wokwi, LTspice, or Falstad screenshots with test results and observations.
06-simulation-validation.md
07
Pitch Deck
3-minute pitch slides. Google Slides link, PDF upload, or markdown slide outline.
07-pitch-deck.md / .pdf
