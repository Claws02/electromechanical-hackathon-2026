How it works

What You Receive at T-0

When the clock starts, each participant is randomly assigned one of five challenge prompts. Below is a complete example of what that brief looks like — your actual challenge will follow exactly this format.

Example Brief Assigned randomly · Revealed at T+0h
Challenge #1

The Forgotten Stove Problem

Unattended cooking is the leading cause of residential fires. Elderly users, busy parents, and anyone who's ever walked out of the kitchen mid-boil are all at risk. Your mission: design a retrofittable smart device that detects when a stove is on and unattended, then safely intervenes — without touching or modifying the appliance.

Target Users
Elderly people living alone, households with young children, or anyone with a history of leaving the kitchen. The device must be intuitive — no app required as a primary interaction.
Device Must…
  • Detect that the stove is actively cooking (heat, humidity, current draw, timer, or visual)
  • Detect that the user has left the kitchen or fallen asleep
  • Escalate: notify → alarm → intervene (your call on how)
  • Reset safely when the user returns or acknowledges
  • Require zero modifications to the stove itself
Failure Modes to Consider
  • False positive: triggers while user is present
  • False negative: stove left on, nothing happens
  • Power loss mid-alert — what happens?
  • User override: how does the user dismiss an alert intentionally?
Your Design Decisions
The sensing strategy, intervention method, MCU choice, power source, connectivity, BOM, and enclosure are entirely yours. There is no single correct answer — judges score your reasoning and execution as much as the outcome.

Schedule

24-Hour Timeline

Four phases over 24 hours, with one live disruption at the halfway mark.

T+0h → T+2h
Research & Ideation
Challenges are revealed and assigned. Define the problem, research existing solutions, sketch initial concepts. Write your Problem Statement (Deliverable 1).
T+2h → T+10h
Engineering Design
Develop your electrical schematic, select components, build a power budget, sketch or model your enclosure. Produce Deliverables 2, 3, and 4.
T+10h → T+18h
Prototype & Simulation
Simulate your design in Wokwi, LTspice, or Falstad. Validate your state machine, check power numbers, and produce Deliverable 6. Incorporate the winning twist constraint from Hour 12.
T+12h · 30-min window
⚡ The Twist Live Vote
All participants vote on one constraint that applies to every design for the final 12 hours. React 👍 to your pick in the pinned GitHub issue. Most votes wins. Voting window is 30 minutes — then it's final.
T+18h → T+24h
Packaging & Pitch
Polish your documentation, build your pitch deck (Deliverable 7), and push all seven deliverables before the deadline. Commits after T+24h will not be judged.
Hour 12

The Twist

16 constraints. 5 voters. One winner — applied to every design simultaneously. The constraint is announced within 30 minutes of Hour 12 and must be reflected in all of your final deliverables.

⏳
Twist vote opens at T+12h
React to the pinned GitHub issue to cast your vote.
