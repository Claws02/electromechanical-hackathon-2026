## Slide Outline

Use this as your script template. Each section maps to roughly 30 seconds.

---

### Slide 1 — Title
**[Project Name]**  
*Electromechanical Design Hackathon 2026*  
[Your Name] · Challenge #[X]

---

### Slide 2 — The Problem (30s)

> "Every [X time period], [user] experiences [problem]. Existing solutions [fail because X]."

- Hook: one shocking stat or story
- Who is affected
- Why current solutions miss the mark

---

### Slide 3 — Your Solution (30s)

> "Introducing [Product Name] — a [form factor] that [does what] by [how]."

- One-line value proposition
- Key differentiator
- Show the device (photo, render, or CAD screenshot)

---

### Slide 4 — System Architecture (30s)

> "The device works in three steps: sense, decide, act."

- Block diagram (from Deliverable 2)
- Highlight the most clever technical choice
- Keep it simple — one diagram, two labels

---

### Slide 5 — The Twist (20s)

> "At Hour 12, we all drew [Twist ID]. Here's how I adapted."

- State the twist constraint
- Describe the design change you made
- Was it a net improvement?

---

### Slide 6 — Demo / Simulation (20s)

> "Here's the device in action."

- Wokwi recording GIF / screenshot
- Or photo of prototype
- Or LTspice waveform + explanation

---

### Slide 7 — Feasibility (20s)

> "This can be built today for $[X]."

- BOM total
- Key components (available at Mouser/Digikey/Amazon)
- Assembly time estimate

---

### Slide 8 — What I'd Do Next (15s)

> "With another week, I'd [most important next step]."

- Top 2–3 improvements
- Path to a working prototype
- Target market / manufacturing volume

---

### Slide 9 — Thank You

**[Project Name]** by [Your Name]  
[GitHub folder link]  

*Questions?*
